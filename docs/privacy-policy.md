## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how K Scan AI ("K Scan," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, stores, and otherwise processes information when you use the K Scan mobile app, related services, support channels, and related websites or hosted pages that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the "Service").

## 2. Age Requirement

K Scan AI is intended for users 18 years of age and older. Users under 18 should not use the Service.

K Scan is not directed to children or minors. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a person under 18, we will take reasonable steps to delete, restrict, or de-identify that information as appropriate, subject to legal, security, technical, backup, fraud-prevention, and operational requirements.

## 3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect:

- Account and authentication information, such as your email address, login credentials, provider-linked sign-in data, user ID, and account status.
- Images, scans, screenshots, uploaded inspiration images, saved style content, Dressing Room content, and related metadata you choose to submit.
- StyleChat messages, prompts, conversation context, saved preferences, and related usage metadata.
- Product, search, shopping, retailer-link, saved-item, and commerce-interaction data.

- Support communications and information you submit when contacting us.
- Website or support-page technical data, such as standard server logs, browser/device data, and basic cookies or similar website technologies where applicable.

## 4. Camera, Images, Scans, and Uploads

K Scan uses your camera, photo access, media access, or upload flows only when you choose to scan, upload, save, or manage images.

You may upload clothing-focused images, outfit references, screenshots, inspiration images, and similar content. Images may be processed locally and/or through secure cloud systems to provide scan analysis, product matching, StyleChat context, Style Library and Dressing Room functionality, shopping assistance, saved inspiration, and related features.

K Scan is not designed for surveillance, facial recognition, biometric identification, or identifying people. K Scan does not create biometric templates, faceprints, face geometry, or identity profiles.

K Scan does not currently apply automatic face blurring, automatic bystander filtering, or automatic PII removal before upload. You should avoid uploading images that include faces, bystanders, license plates, government IDs, financial records, medical information, private documents, or other sensitive identifiers unless you intend that content to be processed as part of the image.

We do not claim that all images stay on device, that no cloud processing occurs, or that a zero-knowledge architecture is fully live.

## 5. StyleChat and AI Processing

K Scan uses AI systems and related service providers to help analyze clothing-focused images, generate fashion attributes, power StyleChat, provide style assistance, support product matching, and improve service quality and reliability.

Depending on the feature and production configuration, AI processing may involve Gemini and/or other AI infrastructure providers. We do not claim that all provider processing has no retention, no human review, no training use, or zero-data-retention treatment unless that configuration is separately verified and disclosed.

Gemini paid-tier status is not verified by this policy. OpenRouter zero-data-retention status is not verified by this policy. Supabase logging, retention, and DPA posture are not fully verified by this policy.

StyleChat messages, prompts, image context, saved style preferences, and related metadata may be processed to provide the feature. AI outputs may be incomplete, inaccurate, delayed, unavailable, or based on similar rather than exact matches.

Do not submit sensitive personal information, health information, financial information, government IDs, or private information about other people through StyleChat or image uploads unless you intend that content to be processed.

## 6. Dressing Rooms, Shared Content, and Messaging

K Scan may allow users to store inspiration, organize looks, save references, send messages, react to content, and manage other user-generated content in Dressing Rooms or related collaboration features.

Dressing Room uploads and related inspiration content are intended to be private to your account or visible only according to the room and sharing controls you choose to use. If you use token-based previews, shared links, or similar sharing tools, people with access to those tools may be able to view the permitted shared content.

Private uploaded inspiration images are not intended to appear in public previews unless a future feature separately discloses that behavior.
