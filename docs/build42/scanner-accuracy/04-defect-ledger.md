## Highest-impact root causes

1. **Ranking evidence is attribute-shaped while real listings are identity-shaped** (B42-SCAN-02).
   It is the reason the actual product can lose to look-alikes, and it is invisible because the
   score is discarded (B42-SCAN-03).
2. **Destination capability of the active provider** (B42-SCAN-01) caps merchant-link validity
   regardless of ranking quality.
3. **No identity producer** (B42-SCAN-04/05): every confidence statement above "similar" rests
   on the vision model's own report, with no independent check.
