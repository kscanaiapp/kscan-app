# App Store Metadata Draft — K Scan (v1.0.0)

> Scope: email/password-only release candidate. All claims below are limited to features present on branch `feature/beta-account-lifecycle`. No third-party login is mentioned or implied.

## App Name

K Scan

## Subtitle (30 chars max)

AI style scan for your outfit

## Description

K Scan turns your camera into a personal style analyst.

Photograph an outfit or clothing item and K Scan's AI analyzes it — identifying the piece, describing the style, and surfacing similar products you can explore.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Scan clothing and outfits with your camera
- Get AI style analysis
- Explore similar product matches
- Save results to your Style Library
- Manage your privacy and your data, including in-app account deletion

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
K Scan uses a simple email/password account. You can review the privacy policy in the app and request account deletion at any time from the Privacy screen.

Note: the first scan after a quiet period may take up to a minute while the analysis service starts up.

## Promotional Text (170 chars max)

Point your camera at an outfit for AI style analysis, product matches, and a personal Style Library. Your style, decoded.

## What's New (v1.0.0)

Initial release: camera scan, AI style analysis, product matches, Style Library, privacy controls, and in-app account deletion.

## Privacy Summary (for marketing/reference — labels are filed separately)

- Account: email address and password (email/password sign-in only).
- Content: scan photos are processed for analysis; saved results live in your Style Library.
- No tracking, no advertising SDKs, no location, no microphone.
- Account deletion available in-app.
